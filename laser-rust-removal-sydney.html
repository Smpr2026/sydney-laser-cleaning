Laser rust removal in Sydney

We take rust off steel of any size with a laser: beams, rails, trailers, machinery, wheels, tools and car panels. The beam lifts the surface oxide and leaves the steel underneath as it was, so the part is ready to prime or weld straight after. No grit, no chemicals and nothing to wash away.

Why use a laser

  • Removes rust without changing the chemical or structural makeup of the steel
  • Gets into pitted rust that sanding and cutting back can't reach (200W pulse laser)
  • No sandblasting media to sweep up and no chemical runoff
  • Precise: one seam, one edge or a whole beam

What we clean

  • Structural steel, beams and rails
  • Trailers and machinery
  • Car panels and classic restorations
  • Wheels, rims, brake drums and parts
  • Tools and workshop equipment
  • Weld prep and mill scale

Which laser we use

For heavy rust on thick steel we use the 1500W continuous laser, which moves fast through big jobs. For car panels, thin sheet and pitted rust we use the 200W pulse laser, which cleans with very little heat so thin metal doesn't warp. Not sure which one your job needs? Questions

Does laser rust removal damage the metal?

No. The laser takes off the surface rust and leaves the steel underneath intact. We match the laser to the job: the 200W pulse laser for thin panels so they don't warp, the 1500W for thick steel.

Is the steel ready to paint after laser cleaning?

Yes. Once the oxide is off, the part is ready to prime or weld straight after.

Can you remove rust on site?

Yes. We bring the machine and an operator to your site anywhere in Sydney. You can also hire a machine and do it yourself.
