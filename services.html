Services

Laser cleaning services

We bring the machine and an operator to your site. The beam takes off the top layer and leaves the material underneath as it was.

Metal

Rust removal

Laser cleaning is fast on metal of any size. It takes surface oxide off without changing the chemical or structural makeup of the steel, so the part is ready to prime or weld straight after. Our 200W pulse laser also lifts pitted rust out of panels that sanding and cutting back can't reach.

Masonry

Graffiti removal

Steam and chemicals often don't get it all, and sandblasting wears down the surface, which is a real problem on soft stone and heritage buildings. The laser lifts the paint and leaves the wall.

Fire damage

The beam reflects off white plasterboard and painted walls while it removes the soot, so restoration doesn't have to mean repainting everything. On this balcony other cleaning methods had already been tried and couldn't shift the soot. The laser lifted it.

Concrete, brick & grime

Fire soot, mould staining and grime off concrete, brick and stone, including overhead work like ceilings and soffits.
