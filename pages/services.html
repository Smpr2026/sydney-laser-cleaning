Our machines

Two lasers, the right one for the job

We run a 1500W continuous laser for heavy work and a 200W pulse laser for fine work. Here's what each one is best at, and what we won't put it on.

On site: the 1500W continuous laser on steel rails
1500W continuous

The heavy hitter

Lots of power, so it moves through big, thick jobs fast. It also puts more heat into the surface, so we keep it for material that can take it.

Best for

  • Heavy rust and scale on thick steel: beams, rails, trailers, structural steel
  • Thick paint and coatings on heavy steel and machinery
  • Fire soot on concrete and masonry, including ceilings
  • Big areas fast: mill scale, weld prep, baked-on grease and oil

Not for

  • Thin sheet metal and car panels: the heat can warp them
  • Aluminium or stainless where the finish matters: it can discolour
  • Timber, soft stone, heritage surfaces, rubber and plastics
200W pulse

The fine one

Short, sharp pulses clean the surface with very little heat, so it's safe on thin and delicate work.

Best for

  • Car panels and classic restorations
  • Pitted rust that sanding and cutting back can't reach
  • Thin sheet, aluminium and detailed parts
  • Stone steps, floors and softer surfaces

How we keep it safe

  • Eye protection. These are Class 4 lasers. The operator and anyone nearby wear laser safety glasses rated for the laser. If you hire a machine, glasses are bought with it, not hired, for hygiene and safety.
  • A clear work zone. We set up a zone around the job and keep people out while the beam is on.
  • Dust and fumes. What comes off is fine dust and fumes, so we keep the area ventilated and protect our breathing.
  • Fire check. We clear anything flammable from the area first, especially with the 1500W.
  • No chemicals, no grit, no water. Nothing to wash into drains and nothing left behind on site.
Metal

Rust removal

Laser cleaning is fast on metal of any size. It takes surface oxide off without changing the chemical or structural makeup of the steel, so the part is ready to prime or weld straight after. Our 200W pulse laser also lifts pitted rust out of panels that sanding and cutting back can't reach.
