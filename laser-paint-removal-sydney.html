Which laser we use

Thin car panels get the 200W pulse laser so the heat doesn't warp them. Thick paint on heavy steel and machinery gets the 1500W continuous laser. Not sure which one your job needs? Send us a photo and we'll tell you.

Questions

Can a laser remove paint from a car without damaging the panel?

Yes, with the right machine. We use the 200W pulse laser on car panels because it cleans with very little heat, so thin metal doesn't warp.

Can you strip powder coat?

Yes. Powder coat and other coatings can be stripped back to bare metal.

Is laser paint removal better than sandblasting?

There's no grit to clean up, no dust through the workshop and the metal underneath isn't worn down by blasting media.
